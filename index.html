GM Chat — Preview

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy describes how the GM Chat — Preview Chrome extension handles information. The extension is provided by Panteon Yazilim Oyun ve Egitim Teknolojileri San. Tic. A.S. (Panteon).

The extension connects to a GameMaster (GM) service chosen by you for chat, notifications, read-status updates and an unread toolbar badge. You can choose to add the active webpage as context for a conversation. It does not record meetings or capture audio, and it does not continuously monitor your tabs.

Information the extension uses

  • Connection settings: the GM dashboard and API addresses you enter.
  • Account information: the name, email address, user ID, organization ID and organization name returned by GM.
  • Authorization information: device approval codes and access and refresh credentials issued by GM after you authorize the connection. The extension does not collect your GM password or copy your browser login cookies.
  • Notifications: notification identifiers, titles, messages, creation and read/seen timestamps, related GM links, and notification details returned by your GM API.
  • Chat: messages you submit, conversation history, assistant responses and related tool results handled by your GM service.
  • Page context: when you choose Add current page, the active page’s title, URL and readable text are collected for a local preview. Form fields, password values, editable regions and hidden text are excluded by the page reader. Visible page text can still contain personal or confidential information. Review the preview before including it in a chat. Browser-protected pages cannot be read.
  • Your read-status choices: opening a notification marks it read, and choosing Read all updates the currently displayed unread inbox items in GM.

The connection request also identifies the client as the GM Chrome extension and includes its version. The extension does not include advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or a general browsing-activity log. Chat processing is described below; authentication credentials are not included in chat messages or page context.

How information is used

Information is used to establish and retain the GM connection, identify your account, provide chat responses and conversation history, use page context you choose to submit, show your notifications, synchronize read status and maintain the unread badge. When connected, the extension normally refreshes the inbox approximately once per minute while Chrome is running. Browser sleep or network conditions can delay this refresh.

The inbox uses GM's existing last-15-days view. An empty or unavailable inbox is reported in the popup. A cached unread count may remain visible during a connection failure and is identified as potentially out of date.

Chat and AI processing

When you send a chat message, GM processes your message and the conversation context needed to answer it. Context may include page text you choose to attach, earlier messages, conversation summaries and results returned by the agent’s configured tools. Your organization chooses the model services and agent tools used by GM. These may include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or another configured compatible model service. The GM server may send this context to those services to generate responses, summaries, titles and follow-up suggestions. Their processing and retention terms depend on your organization’s configuration and agreements; ask your GM administrator which services apply.

Page content is treated as conversation data. Adding a page collects a local preview; the preview is sent to GM only when you include it in a submitted message. No full browsing history, cookies, password fields or browser session credentials are attached. Agent tools can access organization data or connected services according to the permissions configured in GM.

Where information is stored

Your connection settings, account profile, refresh credential and latest notification snapshot are saved in local storage for this Chrome profile. They are not placed in Chrome Sync storage. Short-lived access credentials and pending device-approval state use Chrome's session storage. Storage is restricted to trusted extension contexts.

The extension does not independently encrypt its local storage; protection depends on Chrome profile access and your device's operating-system security. Avoid sharing your unlocked browser profile or device with people who should not have access to your GM account.

The cached notification snapshot is replaced when the inbox is refreshed. It can remain stored while the browser is offline. Account credentials and the cached inbox remain until you disconnect, uninstall the extension, or the extension detects that your session is no longer valid. Saved server addresses can remain after disconnect so that reconnecting is easier. Pending approval information is session-scoped and subject to the expiry returned by GM.

Unsent page previews are kept in the popup’s memory and are cleared when removed or when the popup closes. They are not saved in Chrome Sync or the notification cache. Submitted chat messages, responses and conversation context are stored by the GM service; server retention, deletion and technical records are controlled by your organization. Removing a local preview or disconnecting does not delete an already submitted conversation or any provider-side records.

Connections and disclosure

The extension sends account-authorization requests, chat messages and chosen page context, inbox requests and read-status updates to the GM API you configure. It requests access to that selected host after you click Connect. Chrome's host permission covers ports on that host. Remote servers must use HTTPS; HTTP is allowed only for a GM development server on the same computer through an exact loopback address.

Page access uses Chrome’s temporary activeTab permission and a bundled reader that runs on demand in the selected tab’s main frame. It does not install an always-running page monitor or read other open tabs. The extension does not collect screenshots, page cookies or form input values.

The GM server receives the information needed to respond to these requests, including the connection's network address as part of ordinary network communication. Your organization controls its GM service, account access, server records and server retention. Disconnecting the extension does not delete those server records. Contact your GM administrator for requests concerning them.

The extension does not sell user data or transfer it to advertising or analytics services. We do not use extension data for personalized advertising, credit decisions, lending, or purposes unrelated to the features described here.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Use of information received through this extension is limited to providing the account connection, chat, selected page context and notification features described in this policy and the store listing. We comply with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The configured model and tool services process relevant chat data to provide the requested features. Other transfers outside the GM service you select are limited to circumstances allowed by that policy, such as your explicit consent, a necessary security investigation, compliance with applicable law, or a permitted ownership transfer. Human access is limited to your consent for specific support data, security purposes, legal obligations, or aggregated and anonymized internal operations. The extension has no separate upload or telemetry channel for these purposes.

Your choices

  • Choose whether to add the current page. Inspect its local preview and select Remove to discard it before sending a chat message.
  • Use Settings → Disconnect this browser to remove the saved account credentials and notification cache from this Chrome profile.
  • Remove the extension in Chrome to remove its extension storage.
  • Remove its selected-site permission in Chrome to stop its access to that GM API.
  • Ask your GM administrator to revoke the server-side external session or manage your GM account and server records. Disconnecting this browser is not server-side revocation.
  • Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information associated with the extension. Do not send passwords or access credentials.

Changes and future features

We will update this policy when the extension's data practices change and show the revised date above. Available features depend on the installed extension version and your organization’s GM configuration. We will review these disclosures when model services, tools or data handling change.
